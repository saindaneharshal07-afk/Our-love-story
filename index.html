FROM MY HEART 💌

Mere Bacchiiiiii Ke Naam 💗

Bacchiiiiii, suno na... 🌸

Vaise toh hum roz hi baatein karte hain, lekin aaj kuch special hai... ✨ Aaj main aapse apne dil ki kuch baatein kehna chahta hoon.

Bacchiiiiii, main jab bhi aapke baare mein sochta hoon na, ya aapki pic dekhta hoon, toh mere chehre par ek alag si smile aa jaati hai... Andar se bohot hi zyada khush ho jaata hoon main! 🥺💖

Kabhi-kabhi main aapke saath apna future imagine karne lagta hoon. Aapse shaadi hoti hai, main hamesha aapke saath rehta hoon... Ye sab sochkar itna khush ho jaata hoon ki bata bhi nahi sakta! 💍✨

Hamari first date yaad hai? Woh sach mein bohot hi zyada special hai mere liye. Main use kabhi nahi bhool sakta! Bacchiiiiii, mujhe sach mein kabhi nahi laga tha ki koi mujhe hug aur kiss karega... Ye toh door ki baat hai, maine toh ye bhi nahi socha tha ki koi mujhe itna pyaar de sakta hai. Lekin aapne diya mujhe! 🥺❤️

Aapke saath bitaya hua har ek pal mere liye special hai. Aapki ek smile se main itna khush ho jaata hoon, jitna shayad koi prize milne par bhi nahi hota! 🎁💖

Har baar aapne mujhe support kiya hai. Jab tension hoti thi, tab aapne mujhe shaant kiya; jab main overthink karta tha, tab bhi aapne mujhe sambhala.

Lekin Bacchiiiiii, isi baat ko lekar main overthink bhi karta hoon ki hum ek kaise honge, ya kahin aap mujhe chhodkar na chale jao... Bas aapko khone ka darr laga rehta hai. 💔

Bacchiiiiii, aapko kisi baat ke liye daantne ka mann nahi hota yaar. Lekin jab koi aakar aapko 'I like you' bolkar jaata hai, toh meri haalat kharab ho jaati hai na! Isiliye kabhi-kabhi jealous aur possessive ho jaata hoon. Aapko kisi aur ke saath imagine karna bhi mere liye mushkil hota hai. 🥺

Future mein bhi main aapke saath hi apni zindagi dekhna chahta hoon. Main chahta hoon ki hum dono ek-doosre ko chunein aur hamesha ek-doosre ka saath dein. Aap mere ho aur main aapka! 🔒❤️

Aap mere liye sach mein bohot hi zyada maayne rakhte ho. Aap mere liye bohot important ho. Main aapke liye bohot kuch karne ke liye tayyar hoon, bas aapse door nahi hona chahta. Maanta hoon ki kabhi-kabhi aapko hurt kar deta hoon, lekin main sach mein aapse bohot hi zyada pyaar karta hoon! Aap meri chhotisi cutie Bacchiiiiii ho! 🧸💖

Mujhe ye safar hamesha ke liye chahiye. Jab se aap meri life mein aaye ho, meri life poori tarah badal gayi hai. Main pehle se bohot zyada khush rehne laga hoon. Pehle akela rehta tha, lekin aapke aane ke baad aapke saath zindagi jeene ke sapne dekhna seekh gaya hoon! ✨

Kabhi-kabhi lagta hai ki bas hamesha aapke paas rahoon, har second aapke saath rahoon... Lekin abhi woh time aaya nahi hai. Bas jaldi se woh waqt aa jaaye jab hum har samay saath reh sakein... Koi humein disturb karne wala na ho, aur hamari ek pyaari si chhotisi alag duniya ho, jahan sirf hum dono hon! 🌍💖

Bacchiiiiii, main sach bata raha hoon... Aap nahi, toh aapke alawa koi aur nahi! Mujhe aapse hi shaadi karni hai aur aapse hi karunga... 💍✨
